# Wren CMS Wiki

**Wren is a whole website in one small file** — an open-source CMS whose entire
engine is a single `index.php` of roughly 1,600 readable lines. It uses SQLite
(which it creates itself), writes in markdown, and needs no framework, no
build step, and no dependencies. It is a modern, from-scratch tribute to
sNews, which proved twenty years ago that a CMS could be small enough to read.

This wiki covers everything: getting Wren, installing it, writing and
organising content, images, comments, settings, SEO, theming, upgrading and
troubleshooting. Use the sidebar to navigate.

If you are brand new, read [[Getting Wren]] and [[Installation]] — together
they take about five minutes, most of which is choosing a password.

Wren is MIT-licensed and free forever. Source and releases live at
<https://github.com/Wren-CMS/wren-cms>; the project site (which runs on Wren
itself) is <https://wrencms.com>.
