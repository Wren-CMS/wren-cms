# Troubleshooting

**Article links return 404 after enabling Pretty URLs.** Your host is not
applying the `.htaccess` rewrite (mod_rewrite off, or a non-Apache server).
Untick Pretty URLs in Settings — links fall back to the `/?q=` form, which
works everywhere — and ask your host about mod_rewrite.

**"This form has expired" when saving or logging in.** Your session cookie
went missing — usually a very long-idle tab or blocked cookies. Reload the
page (a fresh form token comes with it) and try again.

**An upload is rejected as too big.** The limit is your host's PHP setting
(`upload_max_filesize`), shown on the Media page. Many hosts let you raise it
in the control panel; otherwise resize the image first.

**Comments never appear.** They are waiting for you — check the admin's
Comments tab. If no form shows on a post at all, check both switches: the
site-wide tick in Settings and the post's own Allow comments tick.

**IndexNow pings don't show in Bing.** Bing's dashboard lags hours behind,
sometimes a day for new domains. If nothing appears after publishing across
two days, your host may block outgoing requests from PHP — ask them, or turn
the toggle off in good conscience; the sitemap still covers you.

**`wren.db` downloads when visited.** Your `.htaccess` is missing or ignored.
Re-upload it from the release zip; on hosts that ignore `.htaccess`, move the
database out of the web root and change the `WREN_DB` constant at the top of
`index.php` to its new path.

**Forgotten admin password.** There is no reset email. If your host offers a
SQLite browser (some do, as phpLiteAdmin), open `wren.db` and replace the
`password` value in the `users` table with a hash generated by
`password_hash('newpassword', PASSWORD_DEFAULT)`. Otherwise upload a one-line
PHP file that performs that update, run it once, and **delete it
immediately** — never leave recovery scripts on a server.

**Something else.** Open an issue at
<https://github.com/Wren-CMS/wren-cms/issues> with your PHP version and what
you expected versus what happened.
